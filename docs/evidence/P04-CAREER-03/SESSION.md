## Career-to-business affinity

`CareerBusinessAffinityPolicy` is a **read-only advisory mapping** in
the Application layer, projected on the Career choice screen:

| Profession | Suggested existing business |
|---|---|
| Developer | Freelance Service; Online Store |
| Marketing | Online Store; Freelance Service |
| Sales | Online Store |
| Office/Admin | Online Store |
| Chef | Home Food Preorder |
| Barista | Coffee Kiosk; Home Food Preorder |

This policy **does not** affect profitability, startup costs, operation
schedules, eligibility, paid bonuses, or save data. Every profession
can still open any eligible business. Making the affinity an economic
modifier would alter deterministic receipts/replay and requires a
separately approved policy/balance contract. This is not misrepresented
as a live economy feature.
